AC Not Cooling? 5 Common Causes and Quick Fixes

Is your air conditioner running but not cooling? Discover the most common reasons, from low refrigerant to dirty filters.

Read More

Dirty filters are the most common cause. Clean them every two to four weeks in summer, as clogged filters block airflow and ice up the coil.

Low refrigerant (gas) is the next suspect. If cooling dropped slowly over a season, a leak is likely and the AC needs leak repair plus gas charging, not just a top-up.

A weak capacitor, a dirty outdoor unit or a faulty thermostat sensor can also stop cooling. If the outdoor fan is not spinning, switch the AC off and call a technician.

Check that the room is sealed and the temperature is set correctly first. If the problem continues, book a diagnosis.

Fridge Gas Charging: Signs Your Refrigerator Needs a Refill

Warm fridge? Ice building up unevenly? Learn the warning signs that your refrigerator needs gas charging.

Read More

Warning signs include weak cooling, a freezer that does not freeze, and a compressor that runs almost non-stop.

Fridge gas does not get used up. If it is low, there is a leak, so the leak must be found and sealed before refilling.

Acting early protects the compressor, which is the costliest part of a fridge.

Washing Machine Making Noise? Here's What It Means

Loud banging, grinding or vibrating can signal drum, motor or bearing problems.

Read More

Banging usually means an unbalanced load or worn shock absorbers. Grinding or rumbling during spin points to worn drum bearings.

A squeal can be a loose or worn belt. A clicking sound may be a coin or object stuck near the drain pump.

Stop using the machine if the noise is loud or the drum wobbles, because bearing damage gets expensive quickly.

Before the cold season, follow these simple geyser maintenance tips to extend its life and cut electricity bills.

Keep the thermostat at a moderate setting. It saves power and reduces wear.
