Installation Quality

Piping, Wiring & Leak Testing

The work behind the wall decides how long your ACs run without trouble

Bulk Copper Piping

Branded insulated copper pipe sets cut and flared to the run length of each unit, with proper bends, clamps, UV-safe wrapping and clean routing along walls and ceilings.

Concealed Wiring & Wall Work

Power and signal cabling with separate MCB per AC, plus concealed copper-pipe and drain channels in walls so the finished room looks tidy with no hanging pipes.

Nitrogen Pressure Leak Testing

Piping circuits are pressurised with dry nitrogen and held under observation to catch joint leaks before the system is vacuumed and charged with refrigerant.

Vacuuming & Drain Lines

Vacuum pump evacuation of every circuit, correct slope on drain pipes and a water-flow check at each indoor unit to prevent leakage later.

Commissioning & Handover

Cooling test of every unit, remote and controller demonstration, and a checklist of all installed ACs handed over to your facility manager.

Stabiliser, Stand & Supports

Outdoor unit stands, anti-vibration pads and cable supports fitted securely on terraces, balconies and service ducts.

FAQ

Bulk AC Installation Questions

What is the minimum number of ACs for bulk installation?
We take bulk and commercial orders starting from about 5 split ACs and going up to 50 or more units. Single or two-AC homes are handled through our normal AC installation service.
Do you install VRF / VRV and ductable systems?
Yes. We install VRF/VRV, ductable, cassette and tower ACs along with their piping, drain and cabling. Share the brand and capacity and we will plan the work after a site survey.
Why is nitrogen pressure leak testing needed?
Copper pipe joints can leak even when they look fine. Testing with dry nitrogen finds leaks before refrigerant is filled, so you avoid gas loss, poor cooling and repeat visits later.
Can you work in phases without stopping my business?
Yes. We plan floor-wise or room-wise work and can schedule noisy jobs for off-hours, subject to your building rules.
Do you supply the AC units or only install them?
We mainly handle installation. You can buy the units from your dealer or brand and we install them. Tell us your requirement on the quote form and we will confirm what we can arrange.
